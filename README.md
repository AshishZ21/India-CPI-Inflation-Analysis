# India CPI Inflation Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) using Excel to understand inflation trends, category-level contributions, food inflation, the impact of COVID-19, and the relationship between crude oil prices and selected CPI categories.

## Objectives

- Analyze the contribution of major CPI categories to overall inflation.
- Identify annual YoY CPI inflation trends.
- Analyze food inflation over a 12-month period.
- Compare CPI behavior before and during the COVID-19 period.
- Analyze the relationship between crude oil price movements and CPI categories.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Pivot/summary analysis
- Data visualization
- Correlation analysis

## Analysis Performed

### 1. CPI Contribution Analysis

Grouped individual CPI categories into broader categories and calculated their contribution to the overall CPI basket.

### 2. Annual YoY Inflation Trend

Analyzed annual CPI averages and calculated year-over-year inflation rates to identify periods of higher inflation.

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis

Analyzed monthly food CPI movements and compared changes across major food categories to identify the largest increases and decreases.

### 4. COVID-19 Impact Analysis

Compared monthly CPI inflation across General CPI, Food CPI, and Health CPI between the pre-COVID and COVID periods.

### 5. Crude Oil Impact Analysis

Analyzed monthly crude oil price movements against General CPI, Fuel & Light CPI, and Transport & Communication CPI.

## Key Findings

- 2022 recorded the highest annual YoY inflation in the analyzed period.
- Food categories showed significant variation during the analyzed period.
- Health CPI showed a stronger monthly inflation movement during the COVID period compared with the pre-COVID period.
- Crude oil prices showed a weak positive correlation with General CPI and Fuel & Light CPI.
- Transport & Communication CPI showed a weaker correlation with crude oil prices.
